ABOUT PSTB TECH

Technology and innovation
at PSTB.

PSTB Tech is PSTB’s student association for technology and innovation.

01 — 05OUR MISSION AND VISIONExplore ↓
01OUR MISSION
AND AMBITION

Make student life a practical extension of academic learning.

Our mission is to build a lasting student community around technology, innovation and collaboration.

PSTB Tech encourages exchange across cohorts and connects academic theory with practical applications. The association complements education, enriches student life and creates opportunities to discover, practise and share.

04LONG-TERM
CONTINUITY

AN ASSOCIATION THAT CARRIES ON

Build for the long term.
Pass it on with care.

PSTB Tech is designed to last beyond the yearly renewal of its student teams.

01 / HAND OVER RESPONSIBILITY

Pass it on

Plan handovers between teams to preserve knowledge, ways of working and established relationships.

02 / KEEP A RECORD

Document

Record projects, events, processes and partnerships so future teams can continue and improve them.

03 / MAKE ROOM

Welcome

Help new members get involved and gradually take on responsibility.

04 / STAY ORGANISED

Plan

Schedule activities, track the budget carefully and set goals that fit the available resources.

PSTB Tech aims to be an association that lasts, passing knowledge and projects from one cohort to the next and encouraging new students to get involved.

05WHAT COMES

GROW AT A REALISTIC PACE

Start small.
Grow together.

PSTB Tech will develop step by step. The first phase will focus on building a community, hosting accessible events and piloting workshops. Later phases may add more collaborative projects, professional talks and partnerships as student participation and resources allow.
