01OVERVIEW
AND AMBITION

Making student life a hands-on extension of academic learning.

Inseme Tech brings PSTB students together around technology, innovation and knowledge sharing.

Our ambition is to build a lasting space for students to exchange ideas, apply the skills they gain in their studies and develop projects together. Beyond technology, the association helps connect cohorts and enrich student life at PSTB.

04LONG-TERM
CONTINUITY

AN ASSOCIATION THAT CARRIES ON

Build for the long term.
Pass it on with care.

Inseme Tech is designed to last beyond the yearly renewal of its student teams.

01 / HAND OVER RESPONSIBILITY

Pass it on

Plan handovers between teams to preserve knowledge, ways of working and established relationships.

02 / KEEP A RECORD

Document

Record projects, events, processes and partnerships so future teams can continue and improve them.

03 / MAKE ROOM

Welcome

Help new members get involved and gradually take on responsibility.

04 / STAY ORGANISED

Plan

Schedule activities, track the budget carefully and set goals that fit the available resources.

This structure helps Inseme Tech keep its identity while adapting its activities to students’ needs and opportunities at PSTB.

05WHAT COMES

Start small.

Inseme Tech will develop step by step. The first phase will focus on building a community, hosting accessible events and piloting workshops. Later phases may add more collaborative projects, professional talks and partnerships as student participation and resources allow.
